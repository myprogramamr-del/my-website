About Us

Two decades of dependable pharmaceutical trading

Al-Marwani Trading Establishment was founded in the capital Sana'a in 2004 under Commercial Register No. 2004-1-309, issued by the Ministry of Industry and Trade — for the import of medicines, medical supplies, electronic devices and equipment, alongside other commercial activities.

After several years of steady growth, we expanded in 2018 into selling and distributing medicines across the governorates of the Republic, becoming an agent for a number of local companies and distributing their products to our customers nationwide.

  • Licensed importer — Ministry of Industry & Trade
  • Sole agent distributor of Natural Pharms products in Yemen
  • Authorized distributor for leading national manufacturers
  • Four branches across four governorates
